# EMBERTHRONE

*The kingdom builds by day. It burns by night. You are both.*

## [▶ Play it now](https://vyva360-byte.github.io/emberthorne/)

**EMBERTHRONE** is a kingdom-defense survival game that runs from a single HTML file. No downloads, no accounts — open the page and rule.

By **day**, you raise a kingdom on a 26×18 map: farms, lumber camps, quarries, markets, towers and walls. Every citizen gets a job, every dawn brings income, upkeep, an event with a real choice, and a weather forecast. Then you sound the horn.

By **night**, you take the field as the Ember King. Your weapons fire on their own — your job is positioning, timing, and the build you drafted. Everything the day built is what the night must defend. Sappers hunt your buildings. Pyromancers set them alight. Wraiths phase through your soldiers. Survive all **20 nights** and face **THE ASHEN KING**.

## The reign

- **13 buildings, 3 tiers each** — houses, farms, towers, catapults and more, all placed by hand
- **A living economy** — gold, wood, food and stone; population, jobs, morale, starvation and fire
- **24-node research tree** — war, craft and faith, from tower engineering to resurrection
- **Seasons and weather** — rain feeds the farms, winter feeds the enemy; forecast at dusk, reckoned at dawn
- **16 dawn events**, each with two choices that actually change the kingdom
- **8 weapons, 8 passives, 6 golden evolutions** — level-up drafts shape every night differently
- **9 enemy types and 4 bosses** — bosses at nights 5, 10 and 15; the Ashen King at 20
- **A king can fall** — he returns at dawn, poorer by 30% of the gold. If the *keep* falls, the run ends… unless your research says otherwise
- **Dynasty progression** — 12 permanent unlocks carried between reigns
- **Seeded kingdoms** — every realm is generated from a seed; share the seed, share the kingdom
- **Endless mode** after the Ashen King falls

## How to play

- **Day:** place buildings, assign jobs, research, repair — the day is untimed. Sound the horn when you're ready.
- **Night:** move with **WASD / arrow keys** (or the on-screen joystick on touch). Weapons fire automatically. Collect XP and pick your draft cards.
- At most 4 weapons and 4 passives, each up to Lv 5 — a Lv 5 weapon paired with its passive unlocks a golden evolution.
- **M** mutes. The game autosaves every dawn and restores on Continue.
